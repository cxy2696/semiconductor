METHOD

Method

This desk is a research tool first and a data product second. The rules below determine every number and ordering you see.

What it solves

It answers three questions within limited time: what state is this stage in, which companies deserve reading first, and is the data behind my judgement complete?

What it does not do

No buy/sell calls, price targets or position sizing. No earnings forecasts. No single-factor conclusions dressed up as answers. No hiding of missing data.

Universe and classification

120+ listed semiconductor companies, classified into 8 stages by primary revenue source. Each company keeps one primary listing to avoid double counting. Additions and removals are recorded in the data files.

Factor definitions

All factors are percentile-normalised to 0–100 within the peer set, then weighted into a research priority score. Percentiles are computed within a stage; stages with fewer than 8 companies fall back to the full sample.

Factor Definition Weight
Quality Percentile of ROE among peers. 0.30
Valuation Inverse percentile of static P/E; P/E ≤ 0 is treated as missing and the remaining weights are renormalised. 0.25
Momentum 60% × percentile of 30-session return + 40% × percentile of 52-week range position. 0.25
Size Percentile of log market cap in USD, a proxy for liquidity and resilience. 0.20 
Research priority = 0.30 × quality + 0.25 × valuation + 0.25 × momentum + 0.20 × size

Data coverage

Coverage is the share of 8 fields present (price, 1D, 30D, market cap, P/E, P/B, ROE, 52-week range): ≥ 87.5% high, ≥ 62.5% medium, otherwise low. Coverage measures completeness, not accuracy.

Market state

Breadth, median 30-day return and dispersion describe structure. Thresholds are fixed and public so calls can be reviewed; the state does not forecast returns.

Research list rules

  • Quality first: quality ≥ 70 and valuation ≥ 50.
  • Valuation reset: valuation ≥ 70 and quality ≥ 50.
  • Momentum leaders: momentum ≥ 80 and 30-day return > 0.

Known limitations

Quotes are delayed or closing prices, with no intraday signal. Static P/E penalises high-growth companies. Revenue growth and order data are missing. Classification involves editorial judgement. FX conversion carries daily error.

Disclaimer

This desk is for industry research and learning only and is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Data may be delayed or wrong; always verify with exchange filings and company announcements.
