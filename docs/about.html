ABOUT

About

A research desk built on public data and public method, so that conversations about semiconductors share a common language.

Purpose

Semiconductors combine high technical barriers, deep cycles and geopolitics. This desk compresses scattered value-chain knowledge, company positions and market data into a verifiable surface, helping readers build structural intuition faster.

Who it is for

Students and researchers learning the value chain, analysts and practitioners who need a fast global view, and individual investors who want transparent data definitions.

Scope

8 value-chain stages, 13 headquarters regions and 175 listed companies across mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, the US, Japan, Korea, Europe and Israel.
